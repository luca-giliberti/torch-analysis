How accurate is it? With scouting from the same event, tested on 166 real matches (each one predicted without its own scouting), the model picked the winner 81–82% of the time, about the same as simply adding up each team's EPA (79–82%). With scouting from other events it is much weaker (67% on 234 matches), so predictions get more trustworthy once you've scouted the event you're at. The win percentages are tempered to match how often it's really right, more loosely for same-event scouting. This section measures it on your own results.
